# Valdia: rebuilt city and royal keep (v357)

The rebuild keeps Valdia at its existing world coordinates, including the old city gate, shop, market stalls, entrance to the keep and royal character.

- The city reuses ashlar/plaster wall bays, timbers, inset doors and windows, roof modules, and interior furniture. The inn, bakery, records hall, clothier and carpenter's workshop are real walk-in spaces; the existing greengrocer remains the working shop. The new rooms are explorable but not new lodging or trade systems. Decorative facades have no door markers. Inside a furnished house, the roof and camera-facing upper walls are cut away, without removing their collision footprint.
- Public plazas and paved streets use narrow-jointed staggered stone; the market has an octagonal ornamental fountain and a series of low lantern posts. Physical road and NPC route coordinates did not change.
- The main keep has a 21×14-tile footprint, four spired towers, coloured stone trim and a walkable ceremonial route from the entrance through the audience gallery to the high dais. The raised levels are continuous terrain sections, not stacked independently explorable floors. Only saves trapped in the rebuilt palace walls are moved to an adjacent safe tile. The king keeps his crown and robe.
- Valdia's ambient wild-spawn exclusion remains in place. No rendering-distance or other region music changes are part of this rebuild.
- The game HTML is the only runtime bundle needed. The existing installable Android package has not been rebuilt or released.
