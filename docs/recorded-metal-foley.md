# Softer player bladed-contact Foley
The unified slice uses only the verified true food-cut recording [Watermelon Slicing 2.m4a](https://freesound.org/people/Yin_Yang_Jake007/sounds/443467/) by Yin_Yang_Jake007 ([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)), from its public HQ preview (source SHA-256 `d93ad007106338f4fb49eb6c0c49d6ee2ff86bc81d424b74dc17a5ea45d79949`). It uses the short recorded 1.290–1.456 s onset before the loud cutting-board action, plus a subdued darker tail from 1.376–1.456 s of the same take. There is no actual injury, separately sampled water, added noise or metal impact in this shared contact. The existing softer metal swing and NPC original metal retain their published recording credits; neither was reprocessed here.

The completed mono 24 kHz signed PCM clip is 4,704 frames/0.196 s; SHA-256 `2da72e0b57ea291c51c8c599980b01e9be9687c11c63a8bb09b93cec7ed54da5`. Measured sample peak 0.52, RMS 0.0676, last 40 ms RMS 0.0082 and 0.031% energy over 3.5 kHz. Existing playback gain .57 leaves additional peak headroom. The old watery slime PCM and unused fallback were removed.

The legacy internal identifier `playerSteelCut` now covers the player's kitchen knife, iron/stone/bone swords and Minotaur axe across monster species. Bladed hits share it; wood, blunt, bare, misses, NPC weapon sounds and damage taken by the player keep the preceding behavior. Actual kills still separately play the common defeat cue once per swing. This change adds no visual effects.
