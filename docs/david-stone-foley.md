# David statue impact and sword extraction
Edited layers from uploader-published sound recordings under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/):
- [stonehit2.wav](https://freesound.org/people/aerror/sounds/350749/) by aerror. Real stone hitting rocks, Zoom H5. The strike crack and small moving chips in both draws. [Publisher HQ preview](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/350/350749_2472895-hq.mp3), SHA-256 `f0acc02d5bcee8620338cb77d3f76e21cdc14476481d404a61823005755bfdee`.
- [Scraping Stone](https://freesound.org/people/dslrguide/sounds/321488/) by dslrguide. Actual metal against a paving slab, Rode VideoMic Pro/Zoom H5. Blade-edge friction in the two separately timed withdrawals. [Publisher HQ preview](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/321/321488_5485024-hq.mp3), SHA-256 `61d82d364bf2c88fc9c6a04b24f187c5f59a597942986f61c4951a6c2d9407a7`.
- [Stone Scrape – Rock on Rock Rubbing](https://freesound.org/people/NikoletB/sounds/846425/) by NikoletB. Dry recorded rock-on-rock texture in the hit tail and two different withdrawal segments. [Publisher HQ preview](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/846/846425_1856262-hq.mp3), SHA-256 `e6887fd65429d5787f812667e200a763f0c3b4639955b03f022cedeb9d8d710f`.
Decoded offline and rendered by `render.py` as different 24-kHz mono masters. Game uses the embedded edits, not remote playback. A material hit plays only for confirmed damage to the temple's actual statue. The two withdrawal sounds are driven by the corresponding actual awakening contacts, and existing effects bus and encounter cancellation remain.
