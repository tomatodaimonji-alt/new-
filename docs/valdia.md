# Valdia: first playable pass

Continue east from Rievel across the river and nature district, then through Valdia's west gate, marketplace and castle bailey. The map is still a square, now 248 by 248 tiles; legacy coordinates and exploration ranks are fixed. No area load or warp separates the new ground from the existing east road. New residents use the existing NPC gait and navigation, with separate daytime stops and stationary/work posts at night. The king's occupation is 王様.

Only the royal hall is accessible in this first pass. Shop buildings and stalls are recognizable landmarks, not yet trading services. The existing watercourse ends in the lake, no replacement dead ends. The next stages are functional markets and interiors; then independent city economy/royal history. Rievel nightlife changes and the unified sacred-temple sight distance remain outside this stage.
