# Valdia: the working market and royal gallery

The market square has six independently dressed stalls: tea, textiles, vegetables, bread, pottery, and provisions. Its look uses visual principles from the official Mabinogi Mobile Dunbarton showcase (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e-kF3mCvuaU) and official gallery (https://mabinogimobile.nexon.com/en/Media/Image): identifiable trades, modest displays with goods and boxes, varied shutters and rooflines. The game uses its own hand-authored geometry and does not include any of their assets.

The southern entrance of the greengrocer is actually open. The upstairs is visual housing, and other scenery buildings have no imitation entrance. Stand in front of the counter and face the shopkeeper during her day shift to buy or sell fresh vegetables. Vegetables restore 18 satiety and do not restore HP.

The royal hall has a continuous physical incline to its 2.4-unit-high gallery, and the king has named, stat-bearing regalia. Valdia's city bounds veto ordinary wild spawning and entry by roaming field monsters, with a small buffer outside the wall.

「王都の午後」 is an original Valdia-only chamber loop; it holds across changes from the market to the royal hall, and the normal exterior resumes outside the city. Source and rendering recipe are included with this change.
