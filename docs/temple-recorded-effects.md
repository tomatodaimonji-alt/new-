# White Ring Temple and Fire Wand recorded effects
The new Foley is cut from four uploader-published Freesound recordings, each released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The loudness shaping and low resonant layers are custom. The eye beam is fictional: its air and flame textures are real recordings, not a recording of a real beam.
- [metal-gate-slam](https://freesound.org/people/newagesoup/sounds/335976/) by newagesoup: gate striking a metal pole in a stairwell. Edited for the blade pass, both blade landings and rumble. Published HQ preview [335976](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/335/335976_4067257-hq.mp3), SHA-256 `711ee4a606a7e593c009a52ebfad5e1d9cb69bd1e9e1dbaa0cef312d030db620`.
- [Closing large metal door-002](https://freesound.org/people/AudioPapkin/sounds/814977/) by AudioPapkin: field-recorded massive metal door. Edited for the two impacts and foot weight; muffled low resonance, not a full door closing sound. Published HQ preview [814977](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/814/814977_8698658-hq.mp3), SHA-256 `ec9849a86fa4c4c1aca763e4ddc76723c5050752665c220a68bb20f1c9eeb922`.
- [Gas Torch Light](https://freesound.org/people/SyntaxSeed/sounds/244229/) by SyntaxSeed: real gas torch recorded outdoors. Selected ignition and sustained flame for the straight beam, sweeping beam and Fire Wand launch. Published HQ preview [244229](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/244/244229_4465058-hq.mp3), SHA-256 `5a7f9baf36cbb291d2c171b4f7e4ae8e2da744569b02438f88937dfaf5d7d5a7`.
- [Blowtorch - Gas out](https://freesound.org/people/Sadiquecat/sounds/707252/) by Sadiquecat: clean pressurized-air recording, supporting blade pass and both elemental textures. Published HQ preview [707252](https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/707/707252_5287430-hq.mp3), SHA-256 `343d9aae6b0933d91f247eb4b920e3f83245138d64ab81b601559880239c3426`.

Sources decoded via FFmpeg. Offline `render.py` verifies source hashes, then edits at 24 kHz mono with extraction, pitch, filtering, short resonance and rounded envelopes; masters are signed 16-bit little endian. The game embeds edited samples only; there are no source or network requests at runtime. The seven edited masters add 24,960 bytes (raw) over the previous six; the follow-up's new 700 ms tail is timed to its existing 680 ms ray and the Fire Wand sound is 300 ms. All are on the existing effects bus. Canceling David's encounter retains the established source-specific short fade, and fire-wand cooldown/charges/projectile damage are unchanged.
