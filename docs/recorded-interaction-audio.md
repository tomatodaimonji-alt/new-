# Recorded interaction Foley

These clips are bundled as mono 24 kHz signed little-endian PCM16 in `world-editor.html`, not fetched at play time. The source times below are measured in decoded source audio. The delivered clips were trimmed, channel-mixed/resampled, band-pass filtered, given short fades and peak normalized. File names denote the actual downloaded formats, not the catalog master formats.

The sword-air recording is excerpted and processed as noted above. Its required attribution is also displayed in the in-game sound panel. The other pre-existing Foley, including the separately mixed coin sound, is unchanged.

For the kitchen knife and iron sword, contact against slime uses this one shared recording instead of the steel clash or the common defeat sample. The recorded swing and other targets are unchanged.

Bow draw is a separate recorded cue at the start of a valid wind-up. The release sound and combat timing are unchanged.
