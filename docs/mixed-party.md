# Mixed adventure parties, v347

The player can have up to three total companions. Summoned monsters and recruited human adventurers share those three slots. A full-party summon leaves the inventory and current roster untouched and announces `これ以上召喚できない。`.

ユリス and カイ can join via `依頼` → `仲間になってほしい`; essential vendors and service NPCs stay in town. The status cards expose individual commands and HP. Monster recall preserves soul durability. Human dismissal returns the original NPC to their life in town; a knocked-out human stays in the roster until dismissed and does not drop a soul. Goblin and Skeleton are additional distinct summonable species, with the same one-soul-per-species inventory rule as existing monsters.

The save retains compatibility with the previous single active soul and persists each member's command mode and HP source. The base, snow-region seam, music and audiovisual settings are not reset.
