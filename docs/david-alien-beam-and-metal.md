# David's metallic sweep and alien eye tone
The forward sweep now emphasizes two CC0 1.0 metal field recordings already verified for the prior build: [metal-gate-slam by newagesoup](https://freesound.org/people/newagesoup/sounds/335976/) and [Closing large metal door-002 by AudioPapkin](https://freesound.org/people/AudioPapkin/sounds/814977/). Their original publisher previews, SHA-256 and edits are in the previous temple effects credits and verified by `render-sweep.py`. A 0.4-second blend, focus on the gate transients with the lower metal door resonance, roll off the upper frequencies and use click-free envelopes. Runtime uses baked 24-kHz PCM; no network request.
David's eye beam no longer includes torch or compressed air recordings. Its original game-specific alien sound is generated from period-matched modulated carriers, cached in a 0.8-second mono Web Audio buffer, and played on the existing effects bus. The one source has a single onset and stays phase-continuous through straight hold, second warning and sweep. Natural ending and interrupted casts fade their own source; normal pause cleanup and a 4-second audio safety cap prevent a lingering tone. No other fire effect or Foley is replaced; the player Fire Wand retains its original v275 real gas-torch master.
