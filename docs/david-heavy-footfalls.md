# David heavy steps
New `davidFootPlant` is a 310 ms mono, 24 kHz PCM edit of two previously credited CC0 records: [Closing large metal door-002 by AudioPapkin](https://freesound.org/people/AudioPapkin/sounds/814977/) and [metal-gate-slam by newagesoup](https://freesound.org/people/newagesoup/sounds/335976/). The preview source SHA-256 and deterministic filter/crop process are checked in `render.py`. Only low contact and room layers are retained, with a little low resonant grounding; no exterior request happens during play. Master SHA-256: `69c7e73802b4f46704384aca18d26ee3497f2ff5453226c05d743f530ac29424`.
