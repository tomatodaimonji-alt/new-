# Fire Wand flare Foley
The previous cast sustained a 300 ms recorded torch/air blend. This cast instead plays a single close, rounded 172 ms ignition burst. The update uses no new external playback or new source licensing:
- [Gas Torch Light by SyntaxSeed](https://freesound.org/people/SyntaxSeed/sounds/244229/) ([CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)): cut and low-pass-filtered earlier brief ignition transient, approximately 4.315–4.386 s, not the old sustained section or any blowtorch air layer. Source preview SHA-256 `5a7f9baf36cbb291d2c171b4f7e4ae8e2da744569b02438f88937dfaf5d7d5a7`.
- Existing CC0 [Wood Knock by Mrguff](https://freesound.org/people/Mrguff/sounds/369710/) and [Door Knock Angry by Julibellule](https://freesound.org/people/Julibellule/sounds/657774/) composite from [recorded combat Foley](recorded-combat-audio.md). Removed upper frequencies and kept a rounded brief body, with three much quieter discrete wood flecks. No synthesizer, sustained air, unlicensed source, or added looping.
The sole changed game asset is `wandFlameLaunch`: 4,128 frames, 24-kHz mono signed PCM16LE, peak .46, 20 ms tail RMS zero after quantization, SHA-256 `5d4cdb5dd4abd03069a825a73fb6ef88ffdc2cb46570f1c398befd996db9b81a`. It triggers normally at accepted launch at the existing .52 SE gain. Damage, flame geometry, cooldown, other spells, Orvas's beam and all mixer behavior remain unchanged.
